Tomorrow the experiment starts.

For the next 16 days, I'm having 100 conversations with Spanish-speaking SMB founders about how they actually run their business in WhatsApp. Not how they say they run it. Not how SaaS surveys say they run it. How they actually do — measured in messages per hour, lost customers per week, the WhatsApp screenshots they show me without prompting.

The hypothesis is something I'm calling **Noa**: that WhatsApp isn't a channel for these businesses, it's the OS — and vertical AI agents get built on it, not retrofitted to it.

The experiment will tell me if I'm right, wrong, or partway between. The synthesis publishes May 17, the same day my a16z Speedrun and YC applications go in. Those are deadlines. The thesis is the real output.

The diary updates daily. The counter updates after every call. The quotes accumulate as founders give me permission to share. The patterns sharpen every two days. If you're a Spanish-speaking SMB founder using or refusing AI tools — book a slot. If you're watching: watch live.
