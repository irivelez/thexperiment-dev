## Day 3 — May 4, 2026 — first interview shows B2B regulated vertical doesn't fit the WhatsApp hypothesis

**What I thought:** WhatsApp is the OS for Spanish-speaking LATAM SMBs. Vertical AI agents get built ON WhatsApp first, not retrofitted to it.

**What this conversation showed:** A 17-year B2B health-staffing operator in Bogotá (10 staff, serves clínicas in 3 cities) doesn't use WhatsApp operationally at all. Their operational substrate is phone + Excel + clinic-private EHRs (Epic-equivalents like Sofía at Keralty, Médicis at others, Salud Tools regionally — none with API exposure). Communication with doctors happens via direct phone calls; scheduling lives in Excel cuadritos. The OS-shaped gap exists, but it's in clinical interoperability and ambient documentation, not in messaging.

**What I'm updating:** The hypothesis may be consumer-facing-SMB-specific. B2B regulated verticals operate inside institutional information systems (or their absence), not on consumer messaging platforms. Reframe to test: the "OS" is whatever information substrate a vertical actually uses today — for consumer SMBs that's WhatsApp; for regulated B2B it's bespoke EHRs + Excel. Still in tension — need 5+ more calls across verticals to know if this is a contradiction or a vertical-specific carve-out.
