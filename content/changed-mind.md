## Pre-sprint pilots — April 15–17, 2026 — Redin signal informs the hypothesis

**What I was wondering before this:** does WhatsApp dominate the operator's whole stack, or just parts of it?

**What these conversations showed:** A Colombian B2B field-services intermediary (10 staff, dispatchable workforce contracted ad-hoc across 8 cities) uses WhatsApp heavily on the customer-facing layer and inside their value chain (intermediaries communicate via WhatsApp + custom platforms). But for contractor self-service, the operator independently chose Telegram, not WhatsApp, when designing their own bot. The customer-facing layer is where WhatsApp dominates. The contractor / internal layer is where alternatives win.

**What I took forward into the hypothesis:** the framing got sharpened to "WhatsApp is the OS for the operator's customer-facing layer," with the contractor/internal layer marked as an open question. That's how I went into Day 0 already mildly suspicious that the OS framing was per-layer, not whole-stack. Day 1 (Valar) then surfaced the regulated-vertical exception, where WhatsApp is absent on both layers. Together these two priors are forcing a rewrite from "WhatsApp is the OS for SMBs" to "the OS is whatever substrate a vertical uses today, and that substrate splits per layer (customer vs contractor) and per regulation (regulated vs not)."

## Day 1 — May 4, 2026 — first interview shows B2B regulated vertical doesn't fit the WhatsApp hypothesis

**What I thought:** WhatsApp is the OS for Spanish-speaking LATAM SMBs. Vertical AI agents get built ON WhatsApp first, not retrofitted to it.

**What this conversation showed:** A 17-year B2B health-staffing operator in Bogotá (10 staff, serves clínicas in 3 cities) doesn't use WhatsApp operationally at all. Their operational substrate is phone + Excel + clinic-private EHRs (Epic-equivalents like Sofía at Keralty, Médicis at others, Salud Tools regionally — none with API exposure). Communication with doctors happens via direct phone calls; scheduling lives in Excel cuadritos. The OS-shaped gap exists, but it's in clinical interoperability and ambient documentation, not in messaging.

**What I'm updating:** The hypothesis may be consumer-facing-SMB-specific. B2B regulated verticals operate inside institutional information systems (or their absence), not on consumer messaging platforms. Reframe to test: the "OS" is whatever information substrate a vertical actually uses today — for consumer SMBs that's WhatsApp; for regulated B2B it's bespoke EHRs + Excel. Still in tension — need 5+ more calls across verticals to know if this is a contradiction or a vertical-specific carve-out.

## Day 1 (continued) — May 4, 2026 — retiring the "WhatsApp is the OS" framing

**What I was claiming in copy:** WhatsApp is the OS for these existing businesses.

**What I actually believe:** the OS is the agentic system that operates the business. WhatsApp is one channel where it shows up, not the substrate. The earlier framing collapsed channel and OS into one thing; that was a copy-level over-claim, not a real prior I was committed to. The canonical source docs (`private/bet.md` for substance and `private/voice.md` for communication) never made this claim — it drifted into the SPINE and propagated from there.

**What I'm updating:** SPINE on the homepage (en + es), patterns text, sprint memory, and the log-call skill schema. The Iván and Redin data now reads less as "contradicting WhatsApp-as-OS" and more as "characterizing the channel landscape the agentic layer has to handle."

**What stays committed:** AI can probably take over part of an existing business, transform it or replace it. Which part, in which vertical, with which workflow, in a way that's cost-efficient, effective, and impactful: the hundred conversations decide. Bias toward 0-to-1 jumps over steady progress when the data supports it.
